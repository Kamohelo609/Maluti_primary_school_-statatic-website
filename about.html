About Maluti Primary School

Where learning meets inspiration, and every child is empowered to reach their full potential.

Our Story

Maluti Primary School was founded with one vision: to deliver quality education to every child in our community. Since our establishment, we have grown into a thriving institution with hundreds of students, dedicated educators, and a proud tradition of academic excellence.

Located in Maseru, Lesotho, we serve learners from Grade 1 through Grade 7. We believe the foundation of a great life is built in the primary years.
